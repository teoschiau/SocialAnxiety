This game is designed as an immersive exposure therapy simulation to help players confront and combat social anxiety in a controlled, safe environment. Leveraging the power of Virtual Reality, the project places the player in realistic, everyday scenarios that typically trigger social anxiety, allowing them to practice coping mechanisms and build confidence across four distinct stages.
The game features **4 levels**, each progressively tackling a different aspect of social fears:

1. **On the Street** – Navigating public spaces, dealing with crowds, and managing the feeling of being observed by passersby.
2. **In the Bus** – Simulating public transit dynamics, close proximity to strangers and crossing the whole public transportation to get down.
3. **In a Restaurant** – Interacting with service staff, ordering food, and managing anxiety while sitting in a busy dining area.
4. **Holding a Presentation** – The ultimate challenge: standing in front of an audience, managing public speaking anxiety, and maintaining focus under pressure.
